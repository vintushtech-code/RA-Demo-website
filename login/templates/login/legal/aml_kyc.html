01 Regulatory Authority & Anti-Financial Crime Mandate

GuardianTree FP and Sabin Balan Finance enforce a strict, zero-tolerance policy against money laundering, terrorist financing, proliferation financing, and financial fraud. We operate in strict conformity with:

  • The Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) and the Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005.
  • The Financial Action Task Force (FATF) 40 Recommendations on combating money laundering and terrorist financing.
  • The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Master Circular on Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards and KYC Norms.
  • Directives issued by the Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND) and relevant international regulatory counterparties.

Zero-Tolerance Anti-Financial Crime Mandate

No client relationship will be established or transaction executed without verified Customer Due Diligence and authenticated source-of-wealth documentation.

02 Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Standards

Prior to onboarding any individual or institutional advisory client, our compliance desk conducts multi-tier Customer Due Diligence:

Client Classification Required Documentation Verification Methodology
Individual / Retail Clients Government Photo ID (Passport/Aadhaar/National ID), PAN card, recent utility bill / proof of address, selfie liveness check. Real-time biometric facial match & CKYC (Central KYC Registry) API query.
High-Net-Worth (HNW) & Family Offices Certified financial balance sheets, tax returns (ITR V), bank statements (past 12 months), trust deeds. Independent chartered accountant attestations & source-of-funds trace.
Corporate & Institutional Entities Certificate of Incorporation, MOA & AOA, Board Resolution for authorized signatories, Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) declaration. Corporate registry verification (MCA/Companies House) & UBO identification ≥ 10% ownership threshold.

03 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for High-Risk & PEPs

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is automatically triggered for:

  • Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs): Individuals who are or have been entrusted with prominent public functions (e.g., Heads of State, senior politicians, judicial or military officials, senior executives of state-owned enterprises), as well as their immediate family members and close associates.
  • High-Risk Jurisdictions: Clients residing in or capital originating from FATF "Black List" or "Grey List" jurisdictions subject to increased monitoring.
  • Complex Corporate Vehicles: Private trusts, offshore entities, or structures with opaque ownership layers.

Establishing an advisory relationship with any PEP requires mandatory senior executive and Managing Partner approval.

04 Source of Wealth & Source of Funds Verification

In compliance with FATF recommendations, we require comprehensive documentary proof establishing the economic origin of the client's overall net worth (Source of Wealth) as well as the specific capital allocated for advisory portfolios (Source of Funds). Acceptable documentation includes audited business dividends, documented corporate sales, inheritance probates, real estate liquidation proceeds, or verified executive compensation records.

05 Global Sanctions & Watchlist Screening

All client names, associated directors, beneficial owners, and authorized signatories are screened in real-time and on a continuous automated recurring schedule against global sanction and PEP watchlists:

  • United Nations Security Council Sanctions Committee (UNSC) consolidated lists.
  • United States Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) lists.
  • European Union Consolidated Financial Sanctions and UK HM Treasury lists.
  • INTERPOL Red Notices and domestic criminal registry databases.

Immediate Sanction Blocking

Any exact match with an active UNSC or OFAC sanction designation results in immediate termination of onboarding, suspension of communication, and statutory notification to FIU-IND without tipping off the target.

06 Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR) & Tipping-Off Prohibition

Our compliance desk continuously audits consultation activity and wealth restructuring requests for red flags (e.g., reluctance to provide CDD documentation, structured transactions below reporting thresholds, unexplained international wire routing).

Where reasonable grounds exist to suspect illicit origins, our Designated Principal Officer is statutorily mandated to file a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) within seven (7) working days. Under Section 12 of PMLA, our personnel are strictly prohibited from disclosing or "tipping off" the client regarding any such report.

07 Record Retention & Regulatory Audit Trail

All client identification files, CDD verification records, consultation transcripts, and correspondence are preserved securely in an immutable, encrypted audit vault for a statutory minimum duration of eight (8) years from the date of cessation of the advisory engagement.

08 Refusal of Engagement & Asset Freezes

GuardianTree FP maintains the unfettered legal right to refuse advisory services, freeze consultation workflows, and decline transactions where a client fails or refuses to provide required KYC documentation, submits fraudulent credentials, or appears on statutory sanction lists.
