01 Fiduciary Privacy Commitment

At GuardianTree FP (operating alongside Sabin Balan Finance), we treat the protection of your personal and financial data as our sacred fiduciary duty. As an institutional wealth advisory firm registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and compliant with international privacy benchmarks including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), we uphold the highest levels of transparency and confidentiality.

Core Privacy Principle

We do not monetize, rent, trade, or sell your personal or financial data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

02 Information We Collect

In order to deliver bespoke private wealth advisory, fiduciary portfolio management, and secure account access, we collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full legal name, date of birth, nationality, government-issued identification numbers (PAN, Passport, National ID), physical residential address, registered email address, and telephone numbers.
  • Financial & Wealth Profiles: Investment objectives, risk tolerance metrics, annual income brackets, liquid net worth statements, banking details, demat account numbers, and sovereign tax residency statuses.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser fingerprint identifiers, login timestamps, operating system specifications, session tokens, and cryptographic hardware signatures used during session authentication.
  • Consultation & Advisory Records: Structured communication logs, booked advisory appointment transcripts, portfolio review notes, and submitted inquiry documentation.

03 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) & Security Logs

To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive financial consultation logs and administrative portals, our platform enforces mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) with time-sensitive One-Time Passcodes (OTP) and cryptographic session validation.

Authentication Telemetry

All authentication attempts, failed password inputs, and 2FA verification requests are logged alongside client IP addresses and cryptographically timestamped to ensure auditability and defend against brute-force attacks.

04 How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly under legitimate fiduciary and statutory legal bases:

  • Provision of Advisory Services: Designing customized asset allocation strategies, financial planning roadmaps, and tax-efficient wealth preservation models.
  • Regulatory & Statutory Compliance: Satisfying mandatory Know Your Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) legal obligations.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detecting anomalous login behavior, mitigating cyber threats, and verifying administrative instructions.
  • Client Communications: Delivering portfolio audit updates, market outlook advisories, and transactional consultation notifications.

06 AES-256 Encryption & Security Standards

Our digital architecture operates on defense-in-depth principles:

  • Data at Rest: All database records, consultation logs, and identity documents are encrypted using AES-256 (Advanced Encryption Standard).
  • Data in Transit: All web and API traffic is secured with TLS 1.3 encryption with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS) and strict HSTS headers.
  • Access Control: Strict Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) and role-based access control (RBAC) governing all internal staff interactions.

07 Your Legal Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, GDPR, and CCPA), you possess sovereign rights over your data:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete copy of all personal records maintained by our firm.
  • Right to Rectification: Demand immediate correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request data deletion, subject to mandatory statutory financial record retention periods (e.g., 5–8 years under PMLA).
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your financial profiles and consultation history in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object & Restrict Processing: Opt out of non-essential communications and restrict secondary data analytics.

08 Data Retention & Secure Destruction

We retain personal and financial records only for as long as required to fulfill the advisory mandate or satisfy statutory regulations. Under SEBI and PMLA statutory directives, client identification and transaction records must be retained for a mandatory minimum period of eight (8) years following the termination of the advisory relationship. Following this retention window, all records are permanently destroyed using DOD 5220.22-M sanitization standards.
