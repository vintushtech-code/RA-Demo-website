11 Investor Charter in Respect of Research Analysts

A. Vision and Mission of Research Analyst

Vision: To protect and serve investors' best interests by providing objective, conflict-free, and professional financial advice.
Mission: To act as a pure fiduciary, educate clients on financial risks and rewards, recommend unbiased low-cost strategies, and maintain absolute transparency in fee disclosures.

B. Rights of Investors

  • Right to get transparent advice aligned with your stated risk tolerance and financial objectives.
  • Right to receive fair, unbiased, and objective financial planning without product sales quotas.
  • Right to receive a complete disclosure of advisory fees and no hidden commission incentives.
  • Right to privacy and confidentiality of personal, financial, and tax information.
  • Right to timely grievance redressal through internal channels, SEBI SCORES, or SMART ODR portal.

C. Do's and Don'ts for Investors

✅ DO's:

  • Always verify SEBI registration status on SEBI portal before hiring an adviser.
  • Pay advisory fees only through transparent banking channels.
  • Ensure a formal written Investment Advisory agreement is signed.
  • Provide accurate risk profile and financial background information.

❌ DON'Ts:

  • Do not deal with unregistered entities or social media tips providers.
  • Do not pay advisory fees in cash.
  • Do not fall for unrealistic or guaranteed return promises.
  • Never share Demat/Trading account login credentials with any adviser.
