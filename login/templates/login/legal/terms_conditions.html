All advice is rendered strictly in a fiduciary capacity in accordance with applicable statutory mandates.

02 Client Eligibility, Representation & Warranties

By accessing our platform, booking a consultation, or engaging our advisory desk, you represent and warrant that:

  • You are at least eighteen (18) years of age and possess full legal capacity to enter into binding financial contracts in your jurisdiction.
  • All information, identification documents, and financial figures submitted during registration, consultation scheduling, or KYC screening are authentic, complete, and accurate.
  • The capital under consideration for advisory services is derived exclusively from legitimate, lawful sources free of any liens, criminal proceeds, or illicit encumbrances.

Fiduciary Suitability Requirement

Our advisors reserve the absolute discretion to decline or terminate an engagement if a prospective client refuses mandatory KYC verification or if the advisory scope falls outside ethical/regulatory parameters.

03 Consultation Bookings, Retainers & Fee Structure

Advisory fees, consultation retainers, and fiduciary compensation models are governed by the following clear tenets:

  • Consultation Bookings: Consultation appointments booked via our online scheduling portal generate a unique 10-character alphanumeric tracking key (e.g., GT7K4M9P2X). Clients may reschedule appointments up to 24 hours prior to the session without penalty.
  • Transparent Fee Disclosure: All wealth advisory fees are charged transparently based on Assets Under Advisory (AUA) or pre-agreed fixed fiduciary retainers. We never accept undisclosed kickbacks or opaque third-party product commissions.
  • Payment Obligations: Invoices are payable upon receipt via approved institutional payment gateways, RTGS/NEFT, or international wire transfers.
