Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a prior commerce or finance degree to join?

No. Our curriculum is engineered from first principles. We start with fundamental accounting equations and build step-by-step into complex DCF models and technical breakout indicators. Engineers, doctors, and non-finance graduates frequently excel in our cohorts.

Does this course guarantee job placement or trading profits?

No. In accordance with strict SEBI regulations and ethical principles, the Kalphora Research Academy is a vocational and educational training initiative. We do not provide job placement guarantees or promise stock market profits. We provide rigorous institutional-quality training, financial modeling skills, and interview preparation for research desks.

How are live demo classes and weekly sessions conducted?

Live cohort sessions are held over Zoom on Saturday and Sunday mornings. All sessions are recorded in high-definition and uploaded to your student portal within 2 hours, accompanied by the lecturer's Excel model spreadsheets and PDF handout notes.

What software and tools will I learn to use?

You will master Microsoft Excel for dynamic financial modeling, Screener.in for custom balance sheet forensic queries, Trendlyne / Tijori Finance for shareholding patterns, and TradingView for multi-timeframe price action and volume analysis.

All lectures, case studies, financial models, video streams, and PDF handouts are designed purely for vocational training and preparation for the NISM Series XV examination.
