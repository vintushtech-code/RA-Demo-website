General Advisory

What services does Kalphora Equity provide as a SEBI Registered Research Analyst?

Kalphora Equity provides independent, non-personalized equity research, stock recommendations (Swing, Breakout, and Long-Term Compounders), sector deep dives, and market educational courses. Under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, we do not manage client funds or provide personalized financial planning; clients execute recommendations in their own demat accounts.

Fundamental Compounders

What is the difference between Fundamental Compounders and Financial Advisory?

Recommendations are delivered in real time through our secure, password-protected subscriber portal. Each recommendation features the stock ticker, entry price range, target price, trailing stop-loss, holding timeframe (e.g. 1–4 weeks or 1–3 years), and detailed fundamental/technical rationales. Subscribers also receive automated alerts on fresh calls, target updates, and exit signals.

Fiduciary

How do you ensure conflict-free advisory and fee transparency?

We operate under a strict fee-for-research model with zero brokerage kickbacks, distributor incentives, or trading commission markups. We charge transparent flat subscription fees for our research, ensuring our analysis is 100% unbiased and aligned with your portfolio performance.

Investment

How does Kalphora Equity protect research reports from piracy?

All downloadable research reports are dynamically watermarked on-the-fly with the subscriber's legal Full Name, Masked PAN, IP address, and access timestamp. Direct links and raw PDF files are completely blocked, ensuring proprietary research cannot be shared without immediate attribution and accountability. portfolios with a minimum monthly contribution of ₹5,000.

Investment

How does financial advisory assist with tax planning and optimization?

Our advisory desk structures tax-efficient portfolios utilizing loss harvesting, capital gains tax balancing, tax-advantaged rebalancing, and optimal instrument selection (such as ELSS, Sovereign Gold Bonds, and direct growth strategies) to maximize your post-tax returns.

Fiduciary

How are my invested capital and personal financial data secured?

Your securities and funds remain directly under your custody with SEBI-registered depositories (NSDL/CDSL). We provide advisory intelligence without taking direct custody of your assets, while all platform interactions are protected using bank-grade 256-bit encryption and ISO-27001 compliant security.
